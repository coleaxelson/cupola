# MediaBrowseScaffold

The library wall shared by Movies, Series, Music and Readarr's Authors: glass app bar, search field, KPI peek, filter chips with a pinned sort control, and a lettered poster wall that becomes the rows of what the field finds while searching.

Rendered from `lib/core/widgets/media_browse_scaffold.dart` (with `lib/core/widgets/search_bar_header.dart`, `lib/core/widgets/media_find.dart`, the A–Z strip `lib/core/widgets/media_browse_index.dart` and the wall's own Wanted chip `lib/core/widgets/media_browse_wanted.dart`; what the field finds is drawn in the rows, headings, skeleton and empty state of [SearchRow](../SearchRow/README.md); the peek is `ServiceKpiPeek`) and Music's bodies from `lib/features/music/presentation/music_find.dart`, Movies', Series' and Authors' from `lib/features/movies/presentation/movies_find.dart`, `lib/features/series/presentation/series_find.dart` and `lib/features/readarr/presentation/readarr_find.dart` (over the shared `lib/features/media/presentation/media_find_bodies.dart`), by `test/design_catalog/media_browse_scaffold_catalog_test.dart`.

- **Page**: an `AmbientScaffold` in the service's accent (the lamp), with the body in the `AppBreakpoints.contentWell` (1140) well. The header (search, peek, chips) sits in a `NestedScrollView`'s header slivers, so it scrolls away with the wall rather than staying fixed.
- **App bar**: `GlassAppBar` (the `GlassSurface` gradient, no blur), `kToolbarHeight` (56), title in `title-large` 600 `on-surface`, one action: `Icons.history_rounded` (tooltip *Activity*) pushes `activityRoute`. While searching a leading `Icons.arrow_back_rounded` (tooltip *Exit search*) clears the query. `showAppBar: false` drops it for hosts that bring their own chrome, and `topPadding` reserves that much space, plus the top safe area, above the header.
- **Search** (`SearchBarHeader`): padding `space-sm` top, `space-lg` sides and bottom. A Material `SearchBar`, min height 52, `radius-md`, `surface-container` fill with a 1px `outline-variant` edge, padding `space-sm`; leading `Icons.search_rounded` inset `space-sm`, in `on-surface-variant` (monochrome, on every screen). With text, a trailing `Icons.close_rounded` clear button in `on-surface-variant`. Input is debounced 400ms. In the desktop shell the field registers as the screen's ⌘F target.
- **KPI peek** (hidden while searching): whatever `kpiPeek` the screen passes, in practice a `ServiceKpiPeek` of the service's figures.
- **Filter strip** (hidden while searching): padding `space-sm` top, `space-lg` sides, `space-md` bottom. Chips scroll, `space-sm` apart: padding `space-sm` × `space-md`, `radius-pill`, `label-medium` 600. Selected: the ink fill (`primary`), label `on-primary` by `ServiceTheme.foregroundOn`. Unselected: the ink at 12% with the label measured against that wash on the page surface: the brand book's chip, the same whichever service owns the library. Chips: *All*, *Available*, *Missing*, *In queue*, *Cutoff unmet* (only when `cutoffUnmetIds` is non-null, and an empty set still draws it), *Unmonitored*. *Available* and *Missing* partition the library through `mediaBrowseBucketOf`; *In queue*, *Cutoff unmet* and *Unmonitored* are orthogonal to that partition. *Available*, *Missing*, *In queue* and *Unmonitored* put a question to each title's status, so they are drawn only when the screen passes a `statusExtractor`: a title with no status reads as a gap, which would answer *Missing* for the whole library and the others for none of it. **Lidarr's wall reads *Wanted* where the others read *Missing*** (`wanted:`, a `MediaBrowseWanted`): *Missing* finds the artists with no track on disk, so an artist short of 30 of its 31 albums sits under *Available*. *Wanted* is the artists with at least one album in Lidarr's `wanted/missing` (released, monitored, nothing on disk), so the row reads *All · Available · Wanted · In queue · Cutoff unmet · Unmonitored*; the two are never offered together. The chip is there from the first frame, so the row does not shift when the answer lands. While it is on, each tile's caption is `3 albums wanted` (`1 album wanted`, the figure bound to its noun) and a line under the chips, `meta` in `inkSecondary`, says `34 artists have a wanted album: released, monitored, nothing on disk.` (`1 artist has`), counted over the artists the wall holds. The walk is capped, and when it stops short every figure is a floor (`50+ albums wanted`, `34+ artists have…`). Selected before the answer is in, the wall waits (a spinner) instead of filtering to nothing; failed, it is the house's `InlineErrorRetry` naming Lidarr and the connection (`…, so the artists with a wanted album are not shown.`), never `Nothing is wanted`, which only a whole read of an empty list says (with *Show all*). A strip with one chip (*All*) draws no chips, as one order draws no sort control; with neither chips nor sort the strip is not drawn at all.
- **Sort control**: pinned right of the scrolling chips and built only when there are two or more orders: *Title A–Z* (always first, the default) plus the screen's `sorts` (`MediaBrowseSort.dateAdded`, `.year`, `.size`, or a custom one). A `PopupMenuButton` (tooltip *Change sort order*) whose child is at least 44 × 44, padding `space-sm`: `Icons.swap_vert_rounded` 18, `space-xs`, then the order's label in `AppTheme.chrome` 600, both `on-surface-variant`.
- **Wall**: list padding `space-sm` top, `space-lg` sides, plus the nav bar's clearance at the foot on a phone. Under a `lettered` order (only Title A–Z) each first letter of the sort title, or `#` for anything else, heads a section in `title-large` 600 with `space-md` below it; sections are `space-xl` apart. Any other order is one unheaded run. While the A–Z strip shows the wall's right margin is 28 instead of `space-lg`. Columns come from `MediaGrid.columnsFor` over the row's width (never fewer than 3), 12 between columns, 16 between rows, and the tiles take the width that is left: about 111 on a 390 phone, about 148 in the 1140 well.
- **A–Z strip** (`MediaBrowseIndex`; Movies, Series, Music, Authors): on the right edge of the body, 28 wide, only while the sort is Title A–Z, the field is empty and the wall shows at least `MediaBrowseIndex.minTitles` (40, about fourteen rows: under that the wall is a flick away). It spans from under the header (and, on a service tab, under the chrome laid over the page once the header has gone) to above the floating bar. Letters `#` first (where the wall heads a digit or anything not a Latin letter: the strip runs in the wall's own order), then `A`–`Z`, `label-small` centred; an accented first letter belongs to its plain letter, in the wall's sort and headings as in the strip (`foldForFind`); a letter with a section in `ink`, one without in `inkDisabled`, and not a target: a finger on it lands on the nearest present letter at or after it (the last one when none follows). Tap or drag jumps the wall at once, no animation, heading at the top of what the body shows (the header folds away; under a bar the heading sits clear of its fade; under a service tab's chrome, just below it). While the finger is down the letter shows large (`screen` role) in a 72 disc to the left of the strip, 24 off it, centred on the letter, `room.menu` fill, `room.edge` line, the float shadow, and a selection haptic fires on each change on iOS and Android. The offsets are measured from the grid's own layout, since the wall is lazy and an unbuilt section has no box. At a larger reading size, when 27 letters no longer fit the height, every other slot is a dot for the letters left out; dragging still reaches each present letter. One adjustable control to a screen reader: `Jump to letter`, value the letter last jumped to, increment and decrement moving to the next and previous present letter. On a desktop it takes a click and a drag.
- **Poster tile**: a 2 : 3 `ContentCard` under its own `Hero`, with an 8px `StatusBadge(iconOnly: true)` dot at 6 / 6 from the lower-left corner; `space-xs`; the caption (`MediaPosterCaption` in `lib/core/widgets/media_poster_caption.dart`, shared with a media server's walls): the title in `label-small` 600 `on-surface` and the subtitle (the year) in `label-small` `on-surface-variant`, one line each, ellipsised.
- **`posterFallbackIcon`**: the glyph on a poster tile with no artwork, the film's clapper by default; Sonarr passes `Icons.tv_outlined`.
- **`tileShape: MediaBrowseTileShape.circle`** (Lidarr's wall): a square `MediaPosterCard.circular` with `Icons.person_outline_rounded` as its fallback, captions centred, and no dot: a circle has no corner to hold one, so the caption carries completeness in words.
- **Finding** (every wall passes a `MediaFind`; there is no other path from the field to the screen): the field's hint names what is found (`Artists and albums`, `Movies`, `Series`, `Authors`). While the field holds text, and where the wall has a service to ask (every wall but Authors), a segmented control sits under it (`MediaFindScopeControl`): `Your library` (the default) and the connection's name (`Lidarr`), two 44 cells in a pill on `fillQuiet` with a hairline, the chosen one on `fillStrong` with the edge, label role, the ink for the chosen and `inkSecondary` for the other. Switching keeps the text; Back and Cancel clear the query as they do on a wall that does not find, and an emptied field ends the search so the next one starts in the library. The scope is a provider the wall passes (`scopeProvider`), so a link can arrive on Lidarr's with a word (Discover's `Search Lidarr for …`). **Your library** answers from the first keystroke from the field's text, with no debounce and no request: the wall's `libraryBody` gets the text as typed. **The service scope** answers once typing has paused (the same 400ms every lookup waits), and `serviceBody` is handed the debounced query; choosing the scope hands it the words as they are at once. The bodies are the wall's, because a result is not the wall's item (Lidarr's wall finds artists and albums). The results are `SearchRow`s (`.artist`, `.album`, and `.title` for the posters of Movies and Series): the picture (a 44 disc, a 44 square or a 2:3 poster, initials without one), the name in the row role, what it is in the meta role, where it stands as a state word under that (the backlog's ring for what is wanted or left alone), and at the end a control when there is something to do: the plus, 44, which is a spinner while its write is out. A row speaks one sentence and silences its parts; the plus is its own button with its own name (`Add Elbow to Lidarr`). `SearchHeading` heads the sections in the label role in `inkSecondary` with the count on the right where the scope knows it (`Artists  2`); `SearchRowsPending` is the skeleton and `SearchNothing` the empty state, the same ones Discover's search draws. A wall that does not pass `find` keeps the grid below, unchanged.
- **Music's library scope** (`MusicFindLibrary`): word-prefix matching on artists' names and albums' titles, blind to case and accents (`bjo` finds `Björk`, `el` finds `Ella Fitzgerald` and `Elephants Dream`), a name the query opens before one it only meets later, each group alphabetical. An artist row: `9 albums · complete` or `31 albums · 64 tracks missing`. An album row: `Artist · 2006 · 7 tracks` (a count never breaks from its noun) and its state in the one word the artist page uses (`Complete`, `9 of 11 tracks`, `Wanted`, `Unmonitored`, the queue's word while it downloads, `Out Fri 2 Oct · not out yet`), the word waiting for the queue before it says `Wanted`. The rows are built as they scroll in (a library of thousands matches `the` in thousands of albums); the heading's count is the whole. An artist opens its page, an album its artist's (Lidarr has no page of an album). The list closes with `Names of artists and albums. Lidarr keeps albums, so a song is found by its album.`; nothing found says `Nothing in your library called “elbo”`, `Lidarr can look further.` and an outlined `Search Lidarr for “elbo”` that moves to the other scope. If the albums could not be read the artists still list, with the app's inline failure and Retry; `Nothing in your library` is never said over a library that could not be read.
- **Music's Lidarr scope** (`MusicFindLidarr`): `GET /api/v1/search`, Artists and Albums, each row held or not by MusicBrainz id against the library. Held: `In your library` (green) for an artist, the library's own state word for an album, and a tap opens the page. Not held: the add disc, and a tap on the row does the same (the artist sheet, or the album sheet). An album the library holds and leaves alone (`Unmonitored`) carries a disc that monitors it and searches (`Monitor Electric Ladyland and search for it`, a snack `Lidarr is looking for …`, or the failure with Lidarr's reason). Asked: `Asking Lidarr` over four skeleton rows. A catalog that did not answer while Lidarr did (Lidarr's 503 naming LidarrAPI): the app's amber failure line (`InlineErrorRetry`: the amber glyph, `Lidarr answered, but its music catalog on the internet did not. Your library still works.`, the amber `Retry`), a fault and not the red of a Lidarr that did not answer. Nothing found: the search glyph, `Lidarr found nothing called “smells like”`, `Lidarr finds artists and albums, not songs. Try the album the song is on, or the artist.` A Lidarr that did not answer is the app's own `AppErrorState` for the service, with `Retry`.
- **Movies and Series** (`MoviesFindLibrary`, `SeriesFindLibrary`; the segments read the connection's title, `Radarr`, `Sonarr`, `Radarr 4K` with two): **Your library** finds by the start of a word of the title or of its sort title, blind to case and accents (`FindIndex`), and draws one section headed by its count and its noun (`2 movies`, `1 series`, `12 series`, the figure holding to the word), each result a `SearchRow.title` (the 44 × 66 poster, its initials without one): `1924 · 45 min` for a movie (the year, the runtime when Radarr knows it), `2015 · 3 seasons` for a series (the year, the seasons when Sonarr lists them; specials are not one), and the word the wall's own badge says it in (`Available`, `Missing`, the queue's word while it downloads; nothing while the queue is still being read: one title, one word on the wall and in the find). A tap opens the title exactly as the wall's tile does. Nothing found reads `Nothing in your library called “zzz”`, `Radarr can look further.` and an outlined `Search Radarr for “zzz”` that moves to the other scope. **The service scope** (`MoviesFindRadarr`, `SeriesFindSonarr`) is today's lookup drawn as the rows Discover's search draws for a title (`CatalogTitleRow`): a title the wall's own library holds (matched by the library id the lookup carries, else by TMDB or TVDB id) says where it stands in the word the wall's badge gives it (`Available`, `Missing` in the ring, `Downloading 45%`, nothing while the queue is unread), the same as in the library scope above it, never Seerr's word or `In Radarr` (the walls are the operator's alone), and offers nothing; one it does not hold is the row Discover draws, with the plus that adds it, asks for it as far as this account may, or says what to set up. A tap opens the title as the wall's tile does. After an add the wall reads its library (and the rail's copy) and Radarr again, so the row turns held in place and the title is on the wall when the field is cleared; the rows stay while it is asked again. A lookup row with no TMDB id that the library does not hold has nothing to add and nothing to open: it is no button. `Asking Radarr` over four poster plates, `Radarr found nothing called “zzz”` under the glyph, and a Radarr that did not answer in the app's own failure state with `Retry`.
- **Authors** (`AuthorsFindLibrary`): a library only. Readarr has no lookup (its metadata host no longer answers), so there is no second scope and no control (`MediaFind.libraryOnly`). The same matching on the author's name, one section headed `3 authors`, each result a `SearchRow.disc` (the photo in a disc, initials without one) with the caption the wall's tile carries (`6/7 books · 1 missing`); a tap opens the author as the wall's tile does. Nothing found reads `Nothing in your library called “zzz”` and offers nothing.
- **Library states**: the wall goes through `AsyncValueWidget`: a centred spinner while loading (the scaffold hands it as `loadingWidget`, in the same ink, as is the pull-to-refresh spinner), on a failure with nothing retained `ServiceOfflineState.failure` when the screen passes `service:` (the cause in plain words, the address, *Retry* and *Settings*, which Movies, Series, Music and Authors all do) and `AppErrorState` with *Retry* otherwise, `NotConfiguredPlaceholder` when the provider throws `<Service> not configured`, and a `FaultBand.unreachable` over the last wall it drew when a refresh fails. An empty library reads *Your <service> library is empty* (the screen's `emptyIcon` over it, and its `emptyMessage` under it when it has one: Authors says where an author comes from); a chip that matches nothing reads its own words with *Show all*. So `libraryProvider` must throw on a failed fetch: one that degrades to `[]` makes a dark server read as an empty library (the Radarr, Sonarr and Lidarr walls did, until they moved to `get…OrThrow`).

The consumer provides `title` (*Movies*), `searchHint` (*Movies*), a `find` (see *Finding*), `serviceName`, optionally `service` (the `ServiceKey`, for the failure leaf), `accentColor`, `emptyIcon` and `emptyMessage`, `activityRoute`, `navigationSection`, the hero tag prefix, the library and query providers, the settings selector for image auth, extractors for title, subtitle, sort title, images, id and status, `onItemTap`, the orders it can answer, the cutoff id set and the KPI peek.

Semantics: a tile is one button named by its full title, with *subtitle, status* as its value (*1922, Available*); the truncated caption and the dot are excluded. Chips are buttons carrying a selected state. The sort control's hint rides on its child (*Sorted by Title A–Z*), because a hint wrapped around a button is dropped. The search field is labelled *Search*.

Do: declare only the orders your model can answer, and pass `cutoffUnmetIds` as null until the service has answered. Don't: pin a tile width: the row resolves the count first and the tiles take what is left.

## States in the catalog

Each sheet is drawn in the dark and the light room; the wall also in a 1280 desktop window.

- The library under Title A–Z, Lidarr's circular wall, no sort choices and no cutoff source.
- A wall with no status to resolve (Readarr's Authors): the sort control alone on the strip and no status chips, the strip not drawn when there is no sort either, and an empty library with its own glyph and line.
- Finding on Music: your library (`el`: two sections with counts, the states, the caption), your library with nothing found, Lidarr with results (held, not held, left alone), asked, the catalog that did not answer, nothing found, Lidarr that did not answer; the library and Lidarr scopes at 200%; the library on a 1280 desktop window.
- Finding on Movies: your library (`sh`: the count and noun, posters with year and runtime, `Available` and `Missing`), your library with nothing found (the Radarr button), Radarr's lookup (held and not held), asked, found nothing, did not answer; text at 200% in both scopes. On Series: your library (`2 series`, seasons). On Authors: the library only, with and without a match (no control, no button).
- The A–Z strip: Lidarr's long wall under Title A–Z (Q, U, X and Z dimmed), the strip under a finger with its bubble, the Wanted chip on (captions and the line), Movies' wall with the strip, the strip at 200% with the collapsed letters, and the 1280 desktop window.
- Loading, a failed load with no service, the same failure with `service:`, not configured.
- Text at 200%: the library wall, a failed load with `service:` and a search.
