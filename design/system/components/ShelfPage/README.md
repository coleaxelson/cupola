# ShelfPage

The page of a person and their shelf: an artist on Lidarr and their albums, an author on Readarr and their books, a person on TMDB and their work. A collection's page is the same room headed by its still (`ShelfPage.banner`). Who they are, what the shelf holds and where each work stands, then what the service does for them and the way to it. The Media page's room, without its slab: a person has no backdrop, their works do.

Lidarr's Library opens an artist's page (`/dashboard/lidarr/library/artist/:id`), Readarr's an author's (`/dashboard/readarr/library/author/:id`), each with the connection in `?i=`; every read and every write goes to that connection. A cast member, a search's person or a title's collection opens a person's or a collection's page beside the title, under the tab it was opened from (`…/media/person/:id`, `…/media/collection/:id`), read from the lens's Seerr. On a tablet on its side one opened from a list goes in the pane beside the list, as a title does (`MediaSplit.pageOf`), and one opened from a title in the pane stays there, Back returning to the title; a phone and a desktop window keep the whole page.

Rendered from `lib/features/media/presentation/shelf_page.dart` (`ShelfPage`, `ShelfIdentity`, `ShelfBanner`, `ShelfCover`, `ShelfWorkRow`, `ShelfSliverGroup`, `ShelfFolderRow`, `ShelfOpenIn`), `lib/features/music/presentation/artist_page.dart` (`ArtistPage`, `AlbumTracks`) with the albums' places and words from `lib/features/music/domain/artist_shelf.dart`, their kinds from `lib/features/music/domain/album_kind.dart`, the pills and the lens in `lib/features/music/presentation/artist_type_pills.dart` (`ArtistTypePills`, `ArtistFindLens`, `ArtistFindTrackButton`), the track finder in `lib/features/music/presentation/artist_track_finder.dart` (`ArtistTrackFinder`) with its matching and words in `lib/features/music/domain/artist_track_find.dart`, `lib/features/music/presentation/lidarr_add_artist_sheet.dart` (`LidarrAddArtistSheet`) and `lib/features/music/presentation/lidarr_add_album_sheet.dart` (`LidarrAddAlbumSheet`), which share `lidarr_add_parts.dart` (the folder and profile rows, the refusal, the missing-folder remedy, the button), with the monitor choices from `lib/features/music/domain/lidarr_monitor.dart`, `lib/features/readarr/presentation/author_page.dart` (`AuthorPage`) with the books' from `lib/features/readarr/domain/author_shelf.dart`, `lib/features/discover/presentation/person_page.dart` (`PersonPage`) with the trades from `lib/features/discover/domain/person_shelf.dart`, and `lib/features/discover/presentation/collection_page.dart` (`CollectionPage`, its tally `CollectionTally`) with the parts from `lib/features/discover/domain/collection_shelf.dart`, by `test/design_catalog/shelf_page_catalog_test.dart`.

- **Room**: `CupolaRoom.ground` under `AmbientBackground`, washed with the light of the first cover the page has drawn (else the person's photo; the lamp until one is read). One column, gutter `AppSpacing.lg`, never wider than `breakpoint.reading-well` (720) and centred in a wider window. Whatever scrolls under the clock fades into the room (`StatusBarScrim`). In a tablet's pane (`MediaPane`) the light comes up from the list's side over its first 110 points, as a title's does, and the back plate closes the pane or goes back in it.
- **Plates**: the back plate (`MediaBackPlate`) on the left and the page's other actions (`MediaPlate`, `more_horiz`) on the right, each 44, `fillControl`, `AppRadius.control`, the edge.
- **Identity**: a 92 disc: the person's photo, or their initials in the feature role on the light at 18% with its edge at 40% (`fillControl` and the edge, unlit). Beside it the name in the screen role, three lines then an ellipsis, and what the shelf holds in the label role at 400, `inkSecondary`: `3 albums on disk · 1 wanted · 1 announced`, `7 books · 6 on disk · 1 wanted`; a figure keeps its word on its line.
- **A person on TMDB**: under the name what they do (`Director`, from TMDB's department) and, beside the summary `1 of 3 on your server`, one 7pt dot per title of the trade they are known for, filled green for each on the server, a ring in `inkTertiary` for each that is not (`ShelfTally`; up to eight titles, past which the words stand alone). Then their trades, the one they are known for first and then whichever of `Directed`, `Wrote` and `Acted` they also did, each a heading with its count over a group of `SearchRow`s (`CatalogTitleRow`): the poster, `2012 · Movie · TMDB 6.6`, Seerr's word for the title, and the control at the end that its card describes. Newest first; a trade longer than ten shows its newest under `Show all 84`. An appearance as themselves (`Self`, a talk show, the news, reality television) is not their work. Then `About`: born, the place, the biography clamped to five lines; then `Credits from TMDB.` in the caption role.
- **A collection**: its still at 16:9, `AppRadius.door`, glowing with the light it gives the room; the name, `Collection · 8 movies · 2006–2022`. Its tally ([CollectionTally](../CollectionTally/README.md)): `5 of 8 on your server`, the strip of its movies and where each stands, and `5 on your server · 2 on the way · 1 waiting for approval` behind their dots. A request for one of its movies that waits for the operator follows as on a title's page (`MediaDecisionCard`, Approve the page's lit fill). Then `In release order`, the oldest first, in rows like the person's.
- **Covers**: on the artist's page, up to three album covers under the name, on disk first and newest first: a third of the column each (at most 160), `AppRadius.poster`, glowing with the light. Without artwork the strip is not drawn.
- **Actions** (the artist's): `Search albums` (Lidarr's search for every monitored album) and `Manage` (to Details), outlined, side by side. The more plate opens `Search releases`, `Import files` and `Remove from Lidarr`; the author's, `Search for every wanted book` and `Remove from Readarr`.
- **The albums**: in four places, each a heading and a group (`ShelfSliverGroup`: `AppRadius.group`, `door`, hairlines, rows built as they scroll in): **Coming** (announced, the soonest first: `Out Fri 2 Oct · not out yet` and `Lidarr looks for it once it's out`), **Wanted** (out, monitored, nothing on disk), **On disk** (a track or more), and **Unmonitored** behind `Show them`. A row (`ShelfWorkRow`): the 64 cover (`AppRadius.chip`; without one the plate, `plateWell` and a disc in `plateDimInk`, dark in both rooms), the title in the row role, `2010 · 5 tracks · 21 min` in the meta role, and the state after its dot or ring (`MediaStateWord`): `Complete` green, `4 of 5 tracks`, `Wanted`, `Unmonitored` with the ring, the queue's word in its tone (`Downloading 60%`). A word that needs the queue waits for it and reads `Unknown` once it failed.
- **Albums by kind** (`ArtistTypePills`, `AlbumKind`): Lidarr gives an album a primary type (`Album`, `EP`, `Single`, `Broadcast`, `Other`) and secondary ones (`Live`, `Compilation`, `Soundtrack`, `Remix`, `DJ-mix`, `Mixtape/Street`, `Demo`, `Spokenword`, `Audiobook`, `Interview`, `Audio drama`, `Studio`). Each album is of exactly one kind, so the counts add up to All: its first secondary type that means something (`Studio` is no kind), else its primary one; an unknown type is `Other`. Pills in a fixed order (`Albums`, `EPs`, `Singles`, `Concerts` (MusicBrainz's `Live`: the Never list keeps the bare word out), `Compilations`, `Soundtracks`, `Remixes`, then `DJ mixes`, `Mixtapes`, `Demos`, `Spoken word`, `Audiobooks`, `Interviews`, `Audio dramas`, `Broadcasts`, `Other` last), after `All`, each with its count behind the label in the tertiary ink (`SelectionPills` with `countBuilder`), in a rail that scrolls sideways and fades at its end while there is more behind it. Only for an artist with more than one kind. A pill is one sentence (`Singles, 4`) with the selected state. Choosing one filters the four places: their headings recount in the kind's noun (`Wanted` `2 singles`, `On disk` `1 single`; `3 albums` over All), a place left empty is gone, and the summary under the name stays the artist's whole. A row of such an artist says its kind after the year (`2010 · Single · 1 track`). The choice lives while the page does. Unmonitored folds into one line, `1 unmonitored single` and `Show them` at the end (one button, one sentence; the count under it at a large reading size), never a heading over a lone row; open, it is a heading and a group like the others. A kind with no noun of its own (`Spoken word`, `Other`) counts `releases`.
- **Find a track** (`ArtistTrackFinder`): at the end of the pills row a 44 lens, `Find a track by Jan Morgenstern`; for an artist with one kind, a quiet text button `Find a track` with the search glyph at the start of the column (no orphan icon). It opens a field (`SearchBarHeader`, 52 tall, the focused edge, `Done` after it) in the place of the pills, and the albums give way to the results; the cover plates and the actions go too. Lidarr lists tracks under albums, so the tracks of the whole artist are read once (`GET /api/v1/track?artistId=`, `lidarrArtistTracksProvider`, mapped where it is decoded) when the finder first opens and held while the page lives. A word typed starts a word of a title, blind to case and accents; titles the query opens come first, the same title newest album first. Under `2 tracks` a row (`SearchRow.album`): the album's square cover (its initials without one), the title with the typed words underlined in the primary ink, `Sintel · track 3 · 5:36` (the length only when known) and the state: `On disk` (green) for a track with a file, else the word of its album's row (`Wanted`, `Unmonitored`, `Out Fri 2 Oct · not out yet`, the queue's word), so a track never contradicts its album, waiting for the queue as the album does. One sentence: `The Dragon, track 3 on Sintel, 5:36, on disk`. Under the list `Tracks of Jan Morgenstern’s albums that Lidarr knows. A tap opens the album on its tracks.` A tap closes the finder, clears a kind that would hide the album, unfolds Unmonitored when the album is there, scrolls the page to the album's row (building it first when it is far down) and opens it on its tracks. While Lidarr is asked: `Asking Lidarr` over skeleton rows; failed: `InlineErrorRetry` naming Lidarr with `Retry`; nothing found: `No track called “zebra”` over `Lidarr knows the tracks of the albums it lists for this artist.` Done, or clearing the field and leaving it, puts the pills back. No lens while the albums of an artist just added are still being read.
- **An album open**: the row opens in place on its tracks (`AlbumTracks`), read on opening: the number, the title, the length, 44 tall with hairlines; a track not on disk is quieter when some are. Under them `5 of 5 tracks on disk`, then for an album with a gap `Search for this album` (the primary) and, for any album out, `Search releases`.
- **The books**: `Readarr is no longer maintained; finding new books may not work. Your books, and searching for a wanted one, still work.` once, under the name: a door (`AppRadius.button`, `fillQuiet`, hairline), the info glyph and the first clause in blue. Then **Books** `by year`, three to a row: each cover at 2:3 (`AppRadius.chip`), or a plate with the title and, under a hairline, the author (`plateSurface`, `plateInk`, dark in both rooms: artwork is not palette); a book not on disk has a dashed edge. Under it its state and its year (`Wanted 1863`, `1864`). Then **Wanted**: a card each, the small cover, the title, `Wanted · no file yet`, `1863 · quality profile eBook`, and `Search for this book`.
- **Details** (one group): `Monitored` (`Lidarr keeps these albums up to date`, `Readarr looks for missing books by this author`), the artist's `Monitor new albums` (`New releases are added and searched for on their own`; on writes `all`, off `none`), `Quality profile` (a picker when there is more than one, asked before it is written), and the folder in the mono role (`ShelfFolderRow`). Each switch shows its new state once the service has taken it.
- **Add** (`LidarrAddArtistSheet`, in `AppBottomSheet`): the sheet a search result Lidarr does not hold opens, never this page: a row with no id has no page. Same chrome and rows as the Media page's [add sheet](../MediaPage/README.md), the connections as pills only when there are two. Header: the artist's photo in a 64 disc (their initial without one), the name in the section role, `Group · Bury, England` (their type, and what tells them from another of the name) in the meta role, what is written of them clamped to two lines, Close in the corner (44, disabled while the add is out). One group of rows, each starting on what the chosen folder's defaults say in Lidarr (`defaultQualityProfileId`, `defaultMetadataProfileId`, `defaultMonitorOption`, `defaultNewItemMonitorOption`): `Folder` (the path in the mono role, the room it has left under it), `Quality profile`, `Metadata profile` (what it keeps under it: `Albums and EPs`), `Monitor` (a picker of Lidarr's seven, each with what it means under it: `All albums` · `Every album, out and to come`, down to `None` · `Lists the artist and looks for nothing`), `Monitor new albums` (on adds `all`, off `none`; `New releases are added and searched for`) and `Search when added` (`Lidarr looks for the albums it monitors`; with Monitor `None` it reads `Needs albums to monitor` and cannot be turned on). A row is a picker only when there is more than one choice. `The folder’s defaults, as set in Lidarr.` stands under the group while the defaults are what is chosen, and goes the moment a row is changed. The button follows the Media add sheet: `Add and search` while Search when added is on, `Add to Lidarr` (the connection's name) when it is off. Reading the options shows skeleton rows and a dead button; failing to read them is `AppErrorState.compact` for Lidarr with `Retry`, and a Lidarr with no root folder or profile says `Lidarr has no root folder yet.`, `Add one in Lidarr, then come back.` and `Open in Lidarr` (its settings); neither draws the button; a refused add stays in the sheet, in Lidarr's own words (`This artist has already been added`) or the classified ones, amber unless nothing answered, and nothing closes the sheet while the add is out. On success: `Elbow added to Lidarr`, the library and the search read again, and the new artist's page opens.
- **Add an album** (`LidarrAddAlbumSheet`, the same chrome): the sheet an album the catalog found and the library lacks opens, from its row or its disc. Header: the cover in a 72 square (`ShelfCover`, a record glyph without one), the album's title in the section role, `The White Stripes · 2003 · Album` in the meta role, Close as above. Under it one blue line (the info glyph, the first sentence in the info tone, the rest in `inkSecondary`, on `fillQuiet` with a hairline), worded so that no verb has to agree with the artist's name: for an artist the library does not hold `Your library doesn’t have The White Stripes yet. Lidarr adds them too, and monitors only this album.`; for one it holds `Your library already has The White Stripes. Lidarr adds this album and monitors it.` Rows for the first case: `Folder`, `Quality profile`, `Metadata profile` and `Search when added` (`Lidarr looks for Elephant right away`), each starting on the folder's defaults (the metadata profile too, `Standard` · `Albums and EPs`: the rest of the artist's discography then arrives unmonitored, and Lidarr's `None` stays one of the choices); the post monitors this album and no other whatever the profile (`addOptions.monitor` `none` with `albumsToMonitor` naming the album, which is what Lidarr's own monitoring reads); for the second only `Search when added`, since the artist's folder and profiles are theirs already (the sheet reads no options and posts the artist as the library holds them). The button follows the artist sheet: `Add and search`, `Add to Lidarr`. Failure to read the options, no root folder or profile, and a refused add read as the artist sheet's do (`This album has already been added`). On success: `Elephant added to Lidarr`, the library read again, the artist's page opens waiting for the albums; if the album went in and the artist could not be read after, the sheet closes and says so all the same, and nothing opens (a write that went through never reads as a refusal).
- **Right after the add** (`ArtistPage(justAdded: true)`, the route's `?added=1`, a query so that a link and a restored route carry it): under the name `Added just now`, and under **Albums** `Lidarr is reading Elbow’s albums. They appear here in a moment.` over three skeleton rows (the cover and two lines, in a group). The page reads the albums again every 3 seconds until some arrive, or for a minute, and then says what it says of an artist with no albums (`Lidarr knows of no albums by this artist yet…`); leaving the page stops the reads.
- **Open in Lidarr**: the label role in `inkSecondary` with the open glyph, centred: the same page in the service's own web app.
- **Remove** (through `showAppConfirmDialog`): `Remove Jan Morgenstern from Lidarr?`, `Lidarr stops looking for their albums.` (`None of their albums are on your server yet.` when nothing is on disk), `Also delete 1.1 GB of files` when there are any, `Don't add them again`; Cancel reads `Keep them`.
- **Twice the reading size**: the name goes under the disc in the room role, the two actions one to a line, a setting's value under its label, and the books two to a row. A cover's lettering keeps the cover's size (it is a picture of a cover); the book's title is written under it instead, at the reader's size.

The consumer provides the record's id and, optionally, the connection (`instanceId`), the library's record to stand in while the page reads its own, and a `heroTag` (the disc takes the flight). Everything else is read: the record, the albums or books, the queue of that connection, its quality profiles.

Semantics: the name is the page's header; a work's row speaks one sentence and is one button when it opens (`Sintel, 2010, 5 tracks, 21 minutes, Complete`), a track speaks `Track 3, The Dragon, 5:36, on disk`, a book `Five Weeks in a Balloon, 1863, Wanted`. The disc, the covers and the plates are silent; the more plate says whose actions it holds.

Do: say where every work stands in the lexicon's words, and let a word that needs the queue wait for it. Don't: paint the service's accent, call a person "it", or offer a search for something the service cannot look up (Readarr finds no new book).

## States in the catalog

Each sheet is drawn in the dark and the light room.

- The artist lit by the first cover: announced, on disk, the settings, the way to Lidarr.
- No artwork: the initials, the plates, the lamp; one album wanted.
- An album open on its tracks.
- The author: Readarr retired, the books by year, the wanted one dashed and searched for from its card.
- Text at 200%: the artist and the author.
- The add sheet: the folder's defaults loaded; two connections, the pills over the rows; the options that could not be read; a Lidarr with no root folder (the sentence, `Add one in Lidarr, then come back.`, Open in Lidarr, no button); the add refused by Lidarr; text at 200%.
- The album add sheet: an artist the library lacks, an artist it holds (the search alone), Lidarr's refusal, text at 200%.
- The artist by kind: the pills with their counts, Singles chosen; the finder with results and the typed words marked, and with nothing found; one kind, `Find a track` in place of the pills; on the desktop shell the pills with the lens, and the finder; text at 200% with the pills.
- The artist right after the add: `Added just now`, the albums being read.
- Reading the artist; a wide window, the column centred.
- A person by Seerr's credits: the role, the dots, the plus to Sonarr, Request, About and the credit line; the plus dimmed where nothing set up can add a movie; at twice the reading size.
- A collection (`ShelfPage.banner`): the still, the slab with the strip, the movies in release order.
- A person in a tablet's pane: Close, the light rising from the list's side.
